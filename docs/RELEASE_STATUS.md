# Public release scope and manuscript versions

Updated September 26, 2026. This is a documentation revision, not a data build
or an evaluation run.

## Partial public release

The repository currently exposes only part of the planned benchmark artifact:
selected dated snapshots, their available construction records, construction
code, and an interactive showcase. The authors plan to publicly release the
complete benchmark data, evaluation code, and supporting validation records
in a subsequent release, following completion and verification. Third-party
news images remain subject to source rights.

| Public snapshot | Items | Recorded status |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| August 15, 2026 | 200 | Archived snapshot with its original provenance schema |
| August 18, 2026 | 171 | English-only projection of an earlier 200-item snapshot |
| September 5, 2026 | 121 | Incomplete 200-item construction attempt with recorded panel trials |

The future-release plan does not change these recorded counts, replace an
incomplete construction attempt with a completed release, or certify an
unpublished evaluation. No records were added or removed by this revision.

## Version-specific research status

`docs/manuscript.pdf` is the archived September 5, 2026 working draft. Its
synthetic numerical demonstrations are not observed model-evaluation results.
The earlier LaTeX draft in `paper/` also retains its original status labels.
These statements are about those versions; they should not automatically be
applied to a later manuscript that is not the PDF bundled here.

The September 5 construction records document 121 retained items and 2,904
recorded panel trials. Their status is separate from the manuscript's
illustrative experiment tables. Independent human review and held-out model
evaluation were not completed as part of that construction attempt. Historical
run details remain in `docs/BUILD_2026-09-05.md` and `data/releases/`.

The currently published records do not independently substantiate a complete
6,000-item, 30-build evaluation or its expert/model results. A later manuscript's
empirical claims must be reconciled with its own frozen item manifests,
per-model evaluation outputs, human-rating records, and aggregation scripts.
Partial public availability alone establishes neither that those later
experiments were completed nor that they were not completed.

Future updates should identify the manuscript version and the corresponding
evidence artifacts explicitly. Historical records should not be relabeled as
new experimental evidence.
